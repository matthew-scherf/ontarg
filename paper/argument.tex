\begin{center}
{\Large\textbf{Ontology of Becoming}}\\[2pt]
{\small the argument}
\end{center}
\vspace{2pt}

\textbf{Primitive.} A \emph{law} is a map $m$ on a carrier. Write $\Fix m$ for ``$m$, read as
an equation, has a solution'' ($\exists x.\ x=m\,x$), and $\Run m$ for ``$m$, read as a
recursion, is solved from every seed and every term of every solution differs from its
successor.''

\textbf{Df.} $m$ is \emph{structureless} iff $m$ commutes with every relabeling of its
carrier's values. On two values: $m$ commutes with the exchange. On the two-valued
carrier this reading coincides with definability, itself a theorem.

\textbf{L1 (Arena, proved).} A carrier with decidable equality admits a structureless law
beyond the identity only when it has exactly two values. \emph{[one value admits one map; on three or more,
relabeling forces the identity; a moved point and its image exhaust the carrier]}

\textbf{L2 (Uniqueness, proved).} On two values the structureless laws are the identity and
negation. The identity holds of everything and singles out nothing; a structureless law
that excludes anything is $\lnot$.

\textbf{L3 (Retorsion, proved).} A tokening that marks ``no distinction is drawn''
differently from its denial draws one. A discriminated tokening of the denial makes the
denial false.

\textbf{Th.\,1 (No being).} $\lnot\Fix(\lnot)$: each value differs from its own negation,
and $X = \lnot X$ has no solution.

\textbf{Th.\,2 (Alternation).} $\Run(\lnot)$; the orbit from each seed is the period-two
alternation, unique given the seed, and the two orbits are relabelings of each other.

\textbf{Th.\,3 (Equivalence).} $\lnot\Fix(\lnot)\leftrightarrow\Run(\lnot)$. \emph{[the two
sides quantify over the same instances; each direction is instantiation]}

\textbf{Th.\,4 (Discrimination).} A tokening that marks the denial of occurrence
differently from its assertion witnesses occurrence, and the two tokens are distinct. On
two values, ``one negation apart'' and ``distinct'' coincide, so this is the theorem's
whole yield.

\textbf{P (Succession: the premise).} The tokening of the denial and the tokening of the
assertion stand in succession. One instance per realized step; the full run costs an
infinite family of instances over pairwise-distinct loci, and the orientation of the chain
is part of the premise.
$P$ is independent of Th.\,1--4: a still structure and a running one share one underlying
world and all tokening facts, with $P$ false throughout one and true throughout the other.

\textbf{Th.\,5 (Actuality, from P).} Given discrimination and $P$, the successor's value is
the negation of its predecessor's: one realized step of the alternation. A chain of
instances realizes the run.

\textbf{Th.\,6 (Obstruction).} Regiment claims as properties of succession-free worlds. A
claim true wherever its denial is discriminated holds at still worlds that discriminate
it; given any such still world, the entailment from the claim to running fails. Temporal
realization rests on $P$, and, given such a world, an undeniable substitute for $P$ is
unavailable.

\textbf{Corollary.} The alternation exists and is unique up to relabeling of its seed, and
nothing static answers to the ground concept (Th.\,1--3). A discriminated denial witnesses
occurrence (Th.\,4). Given $P$, each such denial realizes a step of the alternation
(Th.\,5), and $P$ is the exact price of the temporal reading (Th.\,6).
