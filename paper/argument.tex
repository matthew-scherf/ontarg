\begin{center}
{\Large\textbf{An Ontological Proof of Becoming}}\\[2pt]
{\small constructed with no axioms}
\end{center}
\vspace{2pt}

\textbf{Primitive.} A \emph{law} is a map $m$ on a carrier. Write $\Fix m$ for ``$m$, read as
an equation, has a solution'' ($\exists x.\ x=m\,x$), and $\Run m$ for ``$m$, read as a
recursion, has a solution from every seed and none of them is stationary'' (for all $x$ the
sequence $x,\,mx,\,m(mx),\dots$ is infinite and no term equals the next).

\textbf{Df.\,1.} $m$ is \emph{structureless} iff $m$ is definable from a bare two-valued
distinction alone, naming neither value: equivalently, $m$ commutes with the exchange of the
two values.

\textbf{Df.\,2.} A tokening is \emph{absolute} iff its mark admits no outside: no second locus,
at the one time of the tokening, for its contrast to be realized in.

\textbf{Df.\,3.} $m$ is \emph{the ground} iff $m$ is structureless and non-trivial (not the
identity).

\textbf{Ax.\,1 (Arena).} A carrier admits a non-trivial structureless law only if it has
exactly two values. \emph{[one value marks nothing; three or more collapse every
relabeling-invariant law to the identity]}

\textbf{Ax.\,2 (Uniqueness).} On two values the only structureless laws are the identity and
the negation $\lnot$; the identity is satisfied by everything and so asserts nothing. Hence the
ground, if it exists, is $\lnot$.

\textbf{Ax.\,3 (Retorsion).} The tokening of ``no distinction is drawn,'' as against its own
denial, itself draws one. The premise of Df.\,2 cannot be coherently refused — its negation is
unsayable, not false.

\textbf{Ax.\,4 (No margin).} An absolute tokening (Df.\,2) has no outside at its own moment;
if its contrast is realized at all, it is realized at another time.

\textbf{Th.\,1 (No being).} $\lnot\Fix(\lnot)$. \emph{[the two values are distinct and each
fails its own negation; no state is identical to its own negation]}

\textbf{Th.\,2 (Becoming, uniquely).} $\Run(\lnot)$, and the orbit from every seed is the same
period-two alternation, unique up to which value opens it. \emph{[write down $x,\lnot x,
x,\lnot x,\dots$; uniqueness is induction on the step]}

\textbf{Th.\,3 (Equivalence).} For the ground, $\lnot\Fix(\lnot)\leftrightarrow\Run(\lnot)$.
\emph{[a fixed point is a one-state orbit that never moves; a stalled orbit is a fixed point:
refusing to be and being unable to stop running are one hypothesis, read in two frames]}

\textbf{Th.\,4 (Actuality).} Let a tokening deny that $\lnot$ runs. By Ax.\,3 it is tokened as
against its own denial, hence is a tokening in the sense of Df.\,2, hence absolute; by Ax.\,4 its
contrast is realized at another time; by Ax.\,1--2 the only realizable contrast on two values is
$\lnot$ itself. So the tokened denial of Th.\,2 is, at two times, an instance of Th.\,2.

\textbf{Corollary.} Necessarily, a becoming exists; by Th.\,1 no being answers to the ground
concept; by Th.\,3 these are one theorem; and by Th.\,4 the
becoming cannot be coherently denied without the denial itself being one of its ticks.
