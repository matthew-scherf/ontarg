\begin{center}
{\Large\textbf{Ontology of Becoming}}\\[2pt]
{\small the argument}
\end{center}
\vspace{2pt}

\textbf{Primitive.} A \emph{law} is a map $m$ on a carrier. Write $\Fix m$ for ``$m$, read as
an equation, has a solution'' ($\exists x.\ x = m\,x$), and $\Run m$ for ``$m$, read as a
recursion, is solved from every seed, and every term of every solution differs from its
successor.''

\textbf{Df.} $m$ is \emph{structureless} when $m$ commutes with every relabeling of its
carrier's values. On two values this is commutation with the exchange. On the two-valued
carrier this reading coincides with definability from equality, itself a theorem.

\textbf{L1 (Arena, proved).} A carrier with decidable equality admits a structureless law
beyond the identity exactly when it holds two values. \emph{[one value admits one map;
three pairwise-distinct values force the identity; a moved point and its image exhaust the
carrier; on two, the exchange moves both]} Marking one value leaves the relabelings that
fix it, and the same equivalence holds under that weaker demand: a mark-respecting law
moves the mark, or moves every value, exactly on two.

\textbf{L2 (Uniqueness, proved).} On two values the structureless laws are the identity and
complement. The graph of the identity excludes nothing. A structureless law that excludes
anything is complement.

\textbf{L3 (Retorsion, proved).} A tokening that marks ``no distinction is drawn''
differently from its denial draws one. The conjunction of a discriminated tokening of the
denial with the denial is refuted.

\textbf{Th.\,1 (No being).} $\lnot\Fix(\lnot)$: each value differs from its own complement,
and $X \leftrightarrow \lnot X$ has no solution in $\mathrm{Prop}$.

\textbf{Th.\,2 (Alternation).} $\Run(\lnot)$; the orbit from each seed is the period-two
alternation, unique given the seed, and the two orbits are relabelings of each other.

\textbf{Th.\,3 (Equivalence).} $\lnot\Fix(\lnot)\leftrightarrow\Run(\lnot)$. \emph{[the two
sides quantify over the same instances; each direction is instantiation]}

\textbf{Th.\,4 (Discrimination).} A tokening that marks the denial of occurrence
differently from its assertion witnesses occurrence, and the two tokenings are distinct. On
two values, ``one complement apart'' and ``distinct'' coincide, so this is the theorem's
whole yield.

\textbf{P (the premise).} The tokening of the denial and the tokening of the assertion
stand in oriented succession, the first succeeded by the second and the reverse failing.
One instance per realized step; the full run adds an infinite supply of pairwise-distinct
loci and a discrimination at every step.
$P$ is independent of Th.\,1--4: two structures over one world satisfy exactly the same
claims, with $P$ false throughout one and true in the other, so no claim entails $P$ and no
claim refutes $P$. The independence holds relative to the world signature $(T,h,\mathrm{tok})$.

\textbf{Th.\,5 (Actuality, from P).} Given discrimination and one oriented instance, the two
loci are distinct, the successor's value is the complement of its predecessor's, and the
structure fails to be still. A chain of oriented instances realizes the run.

\textbf{Th.\,6 (Obstruction).} Regiment claims as properties of succession-free worlds. A
claim true wherever its denial is discriminated holds at still worlds that discriminate
that denial; given any such still world, the entailment from the claim to running fails.
Temporal realization rests on $P$, and, given such a world, an undeniable substitute for
$P$ is unavailable. A still world that does discriminate is exhibited by ostension.

\textbf{Th.\,7 (Orientation).} On loci with decidable equality, every relabeling-invariant
succession is symmetric, so no invariant succession holds of a pair while failing of the
reverse pair. Direction is unavailable from invariance, which is where the orientation
clause of $P$ earns its place.

\textbf{Corollary.} The alternation exists and is unique up to relabeling of its seed, and
nothing static answers to the ground concept (Th.\,1--3). A discriminated denial witnesses
occurrence (Th.\,4). Given $P$, each such denial realizes a step of the alternation
(Th.\,5), and $P$ is the exact price of the temporal reading (Th.\,6--7).

\textbf{Ledger.}
(1) \emph{Verified conditionals}: the formal results, with an empty axiom footprint.
(2) \emph{Five regimentation choices}: ground as law, structureless as equivariant,
groundhood as exclusion, satisfaction as objectual or processual, claims as properties of
succession-free worlds.
(3) \emph{One empirical input}: the actual world contains a tokening that discriminates the
pair, discharged once by ostension; the discrimination at every step in item (4) is
empirical too, and it is counted there with the premise.
(4) \emph{The premise $P$}: one oriented instance per realized step; the full run adds an
infinite supply of pairwise-distinct loci and a discrimination at every step.
(5) \emph{A consistent constructive metalogic.}\\[2pt]
